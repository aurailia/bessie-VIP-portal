HOW TO USE THIS ON CAMERA
1
Always start from a question. Before you hit record, read the question out loud, then answer it like the woman who asked is sitting across from you. No blank page, no script.
2
Bring someone into the room. Your voice flows when another person is there. Have a friend, a Portal lady or your VA ask you questions off camera, record a conversation, or go live and answer comments as they come in.
3
Speak like the gatherer. Say "we" and "come sit with us." Invite her into the meditation, the circle, the community. Your Gate 45 voice is at its best when it offers what you have and brings people together.
4
Use bullet points, not a script. Your Spleen gives you quiet, in-the-moment knowing. It speaks once and in real time. A script covers it up. Three bullet points leave room for it, and your first take is often your best.
5
Only record when your Sacral is lit up. Ask "Do I want to record this today?" If the answer is flat, the video will feel flat too. Choose a different question or a different day.
6
Record before you scroll. An open Throat picks up other people's voices. If you watch other creators right before you film, you may catch yourself sounding like them. Record first, then watch.
7
Let silence be. An open Throat can feel pressure to fill every pause. In a meditation or a hypnosis session, the quiet is part of the gift. You don't have to keep talking.

The rose water reset

The practice we talked about at the start of our call. It calms the nervous system fast by pulling you out of fight or flight, and it's lovely on these hot days. Do it midday, or two or three times when it's scorching.

  1. Fill your collapsible bowl with ice and cool water, and add a splash of rose water.
  2. Take a breath, then gently dip your face in for a few seconds.
  3. Come up and take three slow breaths. Repeat once or twice if it feels good.

Make it easy: freeze rose water into your ice cube tray so it's ready to go. Add cucumber slices, or a little fresh aloe from a pot, when you want it to feel more like a ritual. This is also a simple tool to hand your own clients when anxiety spikes. If you have a heart condition, check with your doctor before cold water practices.
